Player Rules & Guidelines

Last Updated: December 2024
General Rules Chart Rules Multiplayer Economy Competition Enforcement
Welcome to NextWave! These rules exist to ensure a fair, fun, and competitive experience for all players. Violating these rules may result in penalties, bans, or account termination.

🎮 General Gameplay Rules

1. One Account Per Player

  • One account only. Multi-accounting is strictly prohibited.
  • Creating alt accounts to farm resources, manipulate charts, or boost your main account will result in permanent bans for all associated accounts.
  • If you need to switch accounts, contact an admin first.

2. No Exploiting Bugs or Glitches

  • If you discover a bug, report it immediately to the admins.
  • Exploiting bugs for personal gain (money duplication, stream inflation, etc.) is a bannable offense.
  • Knowingly benefiting from a bug without reporting it is also considered exploitation.

3. Fair Play

  • Play the game as intended. Use the features provided within the app.
  • Automated scripts, bots, or third-party tools to play the game are prohibited.
  • Any attempt to modify game data, reverse-engineer the app, or access backend systems is strictly forbidden.

📊 Chart & Stream Rules

4. No Stream Farming

  • Releasing excessive songs solely to inflate stream counts is considered stream farming.
  • The game has anti-farming systems that will:
    • Apply diminishing returns to songs released in quick succession
    • Limit the number of actively charting songs
    • Cannibalize streams from your own overlapping releases

5. Chart Manipulation Prohibited

  • Do not coordinate with other players to artificially boost chart positions.
  • "Streaming parties" where groups target specific songs to manipulate rankings are not allowed.
  • Let the simulation run naturally - your success should come from smart gameplay, not manipulation.

6. Authentic Releases

  • Songs should represent genuine effort in the game (recording, production, features).
  • Mass-releasing low-effort songs to game the system will trigger anti-farming penalties.

🤝 Multiplayer Conduct

7. Respectful Communication

  • Treat all players with respect in any in-game messaging or social features.
  • No harassment, hate speech, discrimination, or bullying of any kind.
  • Keep conversations appropriate for all ages.

8. Beef & Rivalry Rules

  • In-game beefs are meant to be fun competitive features, not personal attacks.
  • Keep beef lyrics creative and game-focused - no real-world insults or personal attacks.
  • Excessive targeting of a single player may be considered harassment.

9. Collaboration Ethics

  • Honor collaboration agreements made with other players.
  • Don't ghost collaborators or intentionally sabotage joint projects.
  • Revenue splits should be agreed upon before releasing collaborative work.

10. Label Conduct (For Label Owners)

  • Treat signed artists fairly per your contract terms.
  • Don't poach artists through manipulation or false promises.
  • Label disputes should be handled through in-game renegotiation systems.

💰 Economy & Trading Rules

11. No Real-Money Trading (RMT)

Selling or buying in-game currency, items, or accounts for real money is strictly prohibited.

This includes trading game resources for goods/services outside the game.

12. Fair Marketplace Use

  • Don't manipulate the WakandaZon marketplace with fake listings or price manipulation.
  • Honor purchases and sales made through the marketplace.

13. KazzApp Transfers

  • Only send money to players you know and trust.
  • Don't use KazzApp for money laundering between alt accounts.
  • Scamming other players through fake deals is a bannable offense.

🏆 Competition Rules

14. Award Show Integrity

  • Vote fairly in award shows - don't create voting rings.
  • Bribing or coercing other players for votes is prohibited.
  • Celebrate wins graciously and accept losses with good sportsmanship.

15. Leaderboard Honesty

  • Your position on leaderboards should reflect genuine gameplay.
  • Any attempt to inflate your stats artificially will result in stat corrections and potential bans.

16. Concert & Tour Fair Play

  • Book venues and tours based on your genuine fanbase.
  • Don't exploit booking systems to block other players from venues.

🚫 Zero Tolerance Policies

The following will result in immediate permanent bans:

Offense Consequence
Multi-accounting All accounts permanently banned
RMT (real money trading) Permanent ban + potential legal action
Cheating/Hacking Permanent ban, stats wiped
Severe harassment Immediate permanent ban
Exploiting critical bugs Permanent ban + stat rollback

⚖️ Enforcement & Appeals

Penalty Tiers

Tier Violation Type Consequence
Warning First minor offense Verbal warning, no penalty
Tier 1 Minor violations 24-hour restrictions
Tier 2 Repeated minor / Moderate violations 7-day ban
Tier 3 Serious violations 30-day ban + stat corrections
Permanent Critical violations Account termination

Appeals Process

  1. If you believe you were wrongly penalized, contact admins through the official channels.
  2. Provide your player ID and a clear explanation of the situation.
  3. Appeals are reviewed within 48-72 hours.
  4. Admin decisions on appeals are final.

📜 Terms of Fair Play Agreement

By playing NextWave Music Sim, you agree to:

  • Follow all rules outlined in this document
  • Report bugs and exploits when discovered
  • Treat fellow players with respect
  • Accept admin decisions regarding rule enforcement
  • Play fairly and let your skills determine your success

🎵 The Spirit of the Game

NextWave is about building your music career through strategy, creativity, and healthy competition.

✅ DO

  • Release quality songs and build genuine fanbases
  • Collaborate with other artists authentically
  • Compete fiercely but fairly on the charts
  • Build your label empire through good management
  • Engage in fun beefs and rivalries
  • Help new players learn the ropes

❌ DON'T

  • Try to cheat your way to the top
  • Ruin the experience for other players
  • Take in-game competition personally
  • Exploit bugs or loopholes
  • Harass or bully other players
